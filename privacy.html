Life Cards — Privacy Notice

Last updated 19 August 2026

What this application is

Life Cards is a personal, non-commercial dashboard application built and used by Michael Stanway. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its developer. It displays personal data — including his own bank account information — in one place.

Who is responsible for your data

Michael Stanway is the data controller. Contact: michaelsstanway@gmail.com.

What data is accessed

  • Bank account information — account details, balances and transaction history for accounts held by the developer, retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, a registered Account Information Service Provider regulated by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA).
  • Personal activity data the developer records about himself, such as device usage and health or fitness figures.

Only accounts explicitly linked by the developer to this application are accessible to it. No third party's accounts are, or can be, connected.

Legal basis and purpose

Data is processed on the basis of the developer's own consent, for the single purpose of presenting his personal information back to him. It is not used for profiling, advertising, credit assessment, or any automated decision-making.

How data is handled

  • Bank data is retrieved directly from the account provider via Enable Banking and is held on the developer's own devices and private infrastructure.
  • It is not sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Access credentials are never seen by this application. Authorisation happens with the bank directly, and the bank issues a time-limited consent.
  • Bank authorisations expire and must be renewed periodically, as required by the account provider under PSD2.

Retention

Data is retained only while it is useful to the developer, and is deleted when it is not. Bank access ends as soon as a consent expires or is withdrawn.

Your rights

As the only individual whose data is processed, the developer may withdraw bank consent at any time through his bank or through Enable Banking, and may delete all stored data at any time. Any enquiry about this notice can be sent to the address above.

Changes

If this application's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
