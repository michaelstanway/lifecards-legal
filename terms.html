Life Cards — Terms of Use

Last updated 19 August 2026

Scope

Life Cards is a personal, non-commercial application built and used by Michael Stanway. It is not offered, sold, or licensed to anyone else, and it is not available for public registration or download. These terms govern its use by its developer.

What the application does

It gathers personal information — including account information for bank accounts held by the developer — and presents it in a single dashboard. Bank account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, a registered Account Information Service Provider regulated by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority (FIN-FSA), under an authorisation granted by the developer to his own account providers.

What it does not do

  • It does not initiate payments, transfers, or any movement of money.
  • It does not provide financial, tax, investment, or legal advice.
  • It does not access any account other than those the developer has explicitly linked to it.

Accuracy

Information shown is retrieved from third-party sources and may be delayed, incomplete, or out of date. It is for personal reference only. The records held by the relevant account provider are the authoritative source, and should be relied on instead of anything displayed here.

Availability

The application is provided as-is, with no guarantee of availability or continuity. Bank connections depend on third-party services and on consents that expire periodically and must be renewed.

Liability

The application is used by its developer at his own risk. No warranty is offered to any party, and no liability is accepted for any loss arising from its use.

Ending access

Bank access may be withdrawn at any time through the relevant bank or through Enable Banking, which immediately ends the application's ability to retrieve account information.
